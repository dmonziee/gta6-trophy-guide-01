Road to the platinum — about 200 hours

Difficulty 4/10, one playthrough, no mission replay, no New Game+, no difficulty settings. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them alone. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots + 1 autosave) — with no mission replay, a chapter-start save is your only way back.

  1. Step 1 — Finish the story (60–80 hours)

    Just play the story and enjoy it. The story is divided into 5 chapters. The 100% checklist is not viewable until after the story, but any progress you make during the story already counts toward it. There are no mission gold medals, so you never need to watch for hidden objectives.

    One potentially missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning — she goes to meditate on the balcony. Accept, and do it in Chapter 1. It is unclear whether that meditation is required for the trophy, so do it and keep a Chapter 1 save. Everything else can be done after the story.

    While you play, scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app (hold Up, then R1) and study animals through the binoculars — both count toward the 100% checklist later.

  2. Step 2 — 100% Completion checklist & 70 of 80 challenges (~100 hours)

    After the story, the checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is forgiving — closer to 60% true completion. You only need 70 of 80 challenges, so skip the hardest ones.

    • Scan 200 of 277 base-model vehicles
    • Study 100 of 255 animals
    • 50 of 66 weapons
    • 70 of 80 challenges
    • 100 optional missions (there are 200–300+)
    • 300 stunts (trophy needs 250 of 397)
    • 15 of 18 meditations
    • All 8 bank vaults
    • All 39 gang operations
    • All 15 Target Decks
    • All 12 shooting ranges — 42 gold medals

    The only tasks that need everything are robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests. Most side quests are not needed at all.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and miscellaneous cleanup (~10 hours)

    Strangers & Freaks are not needed for 100%, so finish the 8 strands that count (see the Strangers & Freaks trophy) and clean up whatever is left — a few activities, a couple of odd trophies, and the two not-yet-confirmed ones if methods have surfaced by then.
